# Discovery call checklist

- [ ] Who does this work today, and how many hours a week does it take?
- [ ] Walk me through the last time you did it, step by step.
- [ ] Which tools are involved? Who has admin access to each?
- [ ] What does "good" look like? Show me a great example and a bad one.
- [ ] What number would tell you this worked? What is it today?
- [ ] What must never happen (wrong price quoted, post without approval, broken site)?
- [ ] What languages and markets are involved?
- [ ] Budget range for tools and AI usage per month?
- [ ] Who will own this after go-live?
- [ ] Deadline or event this needs to be ready for?
