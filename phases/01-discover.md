# Phase 1 — Discover

**Goal:** understand the job to be done well enough to describe it better than the client can.

## Do
- Shadow the person who does the work today (screen-share for 30–60 minutes).
- Map the current workflow: trigger → steps → tools → hand-offs → output.
- Collect real samples: 20+ emails, tickets, product pages, leads, whatever the system will touch.
- Find the number the business already tracks (leads/month, reply time, hours/week).
- List every system it touches and who owns access (CMS, CRM, email, WhatsApp, analytics).

## Ask
- "What happens if this goes wrong?" — tells you where human approval is non-negotiable.
- "Who will look after this after go-live?" — tells you how simple the handoff must be.
- "What have you already tried?" — saves you from rebuilding a failed idea.

## Output
- Problem statement (3 sentences) and one success metric with a baseline.
- Workflow map and a list of systems + access owners.
