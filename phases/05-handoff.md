# Phase 5 — Hand off & learn

**Goal:** the client owns the system, and what you learned improves the next deployment.

## Do
- Deliver a runbook: how it works, how to approve, how to pause, who to call, common failures.
- Review the success metric against the baseline with the client after 2 and 6 weeks.
- Record every workaround you had to build; these are product gaps.
- Turn repeatable pieces into reusable modules or open-source tools.

## Output
Runbook, metric review, and a short "what we learned" note feeding the product roadmap.
